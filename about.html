Our story

One advisor. One plan. A vision for lifelong wealth.

In 2011, Michael Laurent founded Stackly Wealth in a New York advisory practice with a transparent fee model, a small roster of families, and a conviction that independent fiduciary advice could rival the big wirehouses—without the commissions, conflicts, and fragmented service that frustrate investors.

Families started with disciplined portfolio construction. Growing estates needed tax-aware strategy. Multi-generational wealth required estate, trust, and succession coordination. Within fifteen years we scaled to 180 advisors, $2.4B in assets under management, and clients across the country—without losing the rigorous research and fiduciary standard that defined our first engagement.

Every recommendation is documented, every fee is disclosed in advance, and every trade is aligned to your written plan before it reaches the market.
  • Fiduciary advisors on every portfolio and planning engagement
  • Transparent fee-only model with role-based access and audit-ready reports
  • $2.4B managed across investment, retirement, estate, and tax strategy
